The Vancouver Capsule

Season 01, worn the way
the crews wear it

A lookbook is the fastest way to see how a kit actually behaves — how the jersey sits under a hood, how the pants move through a set, which layers hold up between the warehouse and the stage. So we shot the season the same way we shoot the league: on the athletes, in the rooms they train in.

Call time is the tee

The Series Tee goes on first — cut long, dropped shoulder, made to layer under everything that follows. Nothing in the way, nothing to think about.

The vest earns the set

By the second run the Utility Vest does the quiet work — pockets that hold their line, a hem that stays put when the choreography drops to the floor.

One pant, two silhouettes

The Convertible Pant zips from full length to short between takes — one piece carrying the whole session, warm-up to wrap, without a change of clothes.

The jersey holds the cold

The Long Sleeve Jersey runs the whole shoot — over the tee at call time, under the vest through the set, on its own once the warehouse cools down. One layer, every scene.
